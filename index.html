Neon Gateway

The police are chasing you through a city at night. Reach the green beam of light to break out – it gets harder every level. Drifting earns extra points. Steer with A/D or the arrow keys, accelerate with W, brake with S, drift with Shift and use nitro with Space (it fills up while you drift). Collect the green repair crystals.

Stay alive. There’s more coming than just police cars...
